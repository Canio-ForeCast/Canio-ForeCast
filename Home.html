Weather affects every part of our daily lives, from the clothes we wear to the journeys we take. Staying informed helps you plan better, travel safely, and make smarter decisions throughout the day.


Monsoon

Monsoon is one of the most important weather seasons, bringing much-needed rainfall that supports agriculture, replenishes rivers, and cools the environment. While it provides relief from summer heat, heavy rainfall can also lead to waterlogging, flooding, and travel disruptions. Staying informed about monsoon conditions helps people plan their daily activities safely and efficiently.
